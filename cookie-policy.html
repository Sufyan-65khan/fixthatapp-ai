What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). They can also be "first-party cookies" (set by the website you're visiting) or "third-party cookies" (set by a different domain, typically for advertising or analytics).

Cookies We Use

1. Essential / Functional Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly. They cannot be switched off in our system.

  • fta_cookie_consent — A first-party cookie that stores your cookie consent choice (accepted or rejected). This cookie expires after 12 months. Without it, we cannot remember your preference between visits.
  • theme — A first-party cookie (stored in localStorage) that remembers your light/dark mode preference across tool pages. No personal data is stored.

2. Advertising Cookies — Google AdSense

FixThatApp uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. This is how the site is funded — there are no subscription fees or paywalls. Google AdSense uses cookies to:

  • Serve ads relevant to your interests based on your browsing history across the web
  • Limit how often you see the same advertisement
  • Measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns
  • Prevent fraudulent ad clicks

The primary AdSense cookies set by Google include __gads, __gpi, and IDE. These are third-party cookies set by Google's domain (doubleclick.net, google.com) and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

If you click "Reject" on our cookie banner: We instruct Google AdSense to serve non-personalised ads only. This means ads are shown based on the content of the page rather than your browsing history. You may still see ads, but they will not be targeted based on your past activity.

How We Implement Cookie Consent

When you first visit FixThatApp, a cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen. You can:

  • Accept — All cookies are enabled, including personalised advertising cookies from Google AdSense.
  • Reject — Only essential cookies are used. AdSense is instructed via requestNonPersonalizedAds = 1 to serve non-personalised ads. Third-party tracking cookies for ad personalisation are not set.

Your choice is stored in the fta_cookie_consent cookie for 12 months. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site.

Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete individual cookies or all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies entirely
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being placed

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari.

Note: disabling cookies may affect how the site functions. Some preference settings (dark mode, consent choice) will not be remembered between visits.
